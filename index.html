The story

After his defeat at the New Year's concert, the Wolf sneaks into the House of Young Technicians to steal a laser net. In the scuffle over an experimental teleporter, he kicks the main console. BOOM: six Golden Gears fly into time-rifts, and both rivals are sucked into the vortex.

To fix the machine and get back to Moscow, they need all six gears. The Wolf is quietly planning to trap the Hare in every era. Of course.

Six worlds

World 1

Ostankino TV Studio

Sneak past Hippo guards through a cooking show, a fencing duel and a rock concert, then outrun a live broadcast chase.

World 2

1980 Olympic Village

Velodrome race, weightlifting, gymnastics beams, then a boxing match where the Hare pulls the ropes from the rafters.

World 3

Russian Fairytales

Wake flying carpets with magic apples, rescue the Wolf from Baba Yaga's dungeon, then battle three-headed Zmey Gorynych.

World 4

Savage Tropical Island

The tribe thinks the Hare is their king. Pass the trials, ride a wild boar, and untie the Wolf before the volcano erupts.

World 5

Midnight Museum & Circus

The Wolf is stuck in cursed armor (clank!). Hide in sarcophagi, cross a tightrope above lions, fire yourself from a cannon.

Final

The Dacha & the Robot Apocalypse

An army of Robot Hares and a 50-foot Mega-Robot. Climb into the combine harvester and fight back, together.

Features

Two heroes, one team

Hare: double jump, wall-jump, sneak. Wolf: heavy, smashes crates, grapples across gaps, wears disguises. Swap any time with Tab and solve puzzles together.

Open-zone worlds

Each world is a hub with objectives, guards, secret routes and challenge pads (races, rhythm boxing, boss fights).

100 Golden Carrots

Hidden across all six worlds. Collect them all to unlock Retro Cinema, a pixel-style egg-catching bonus game.

2-player versus

Cat & Mouse on one keyboard, split-screen: the Hare grabs carrots, the Wolf tags.

Four cinematics

Dialogue-free slapstick cutscenes in the spirit of the cartoon: the break-in, the royal misunderstanding, the invasion, and a very wet ending.
